# Creating Soundwalks

The [RWA Creator](../rwa-creator/index.md) and [RWA Player](../rwa-player/index.md) manuals document the two
applications view by view. This section is about the work *between* them: the artistic, creative and technical
process of actually crafting a soundwalk.

A soundwalk lives at the cross-section of reality and fiction. Through open headphones, the real sounds of the
place blend with fieldrecordings, compositions and synthesized material that you place virtually into the
environment. Getting this blend right is a craft of its own: it happens in preproduction, in the studio, and -
crucially - *outdoors, on location*. The guides in this section follow that work wherever it takes place, across
both applications and into the field.

## Location Data & Field Work

Much of the craft only reveals itself on location: how far away things *sound*, what the GPS accuracy really is
between those two buildings, whether a state boundary sits where your feet expect it. RWA Creator and RWA Player
can exchange location data over WiFi - in both directions - which makes this field work fast and playful:

- [Simulating Location on the Phone](./simulating-location-on-the-phone.md): test a soundwalk running on the
  iPhone while steering the Hero from RWA Creator, without being on site.
- [Live GPS in RWA Creator](./live-gps-in-rwa-creator.md): take the laptop outside and design sounds on
  location, with the Hero following the iPhone's GPS position in realtime.
